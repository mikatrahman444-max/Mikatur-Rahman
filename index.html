Why Stores Lose Time & Sales

Manual support slows your store down

Most online stores lose hours and ready-to-buy shoppers to the same three daily bottlenecks.

01

Repeated customer questions

"Where is my order?", "How long is shipping?", and sizing questions flood your inbox every day and pull you away from growth.

02

Slow replies lose buyers

Shoppers who wait hours for a simple answer while browsing at night usually close the tab and buy somewhere else.
